## Power BI: Performance Driver Analysis

### Objective

The purpose of this dashboard is to identify the factors driving business performance and profitability.

While the Excel dashboard focuses on overall financial health, this Power BI dashboard explores the operational drivers behind those results by analyzing revenue generation, expense allocation, product performance, and category trends.

### Key Metrics

- Total Revenue
- Net Operating Profit
- Profit Margin
- Fee Burden Ratio

### Areas of Analysis

#### Revenue Drivers

- Total Revenue by Category
- Revenue Contribution by Product

#### Cost Drivers

- Expense Distribution by Expense Type
- Fee Burden Analysis

#### Performance Trends

- Monthly Revenue Trend by Category

### Business Questions

- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products contribute most to sales?
- What expenses have the greatest impact on profitability?
- How has category revenue changed over time?
- What factors are driving overall business performance?
