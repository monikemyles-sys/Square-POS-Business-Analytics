# Square POS Business Analytics

## Project Overview

This repository documents my journey of learning data analytics through the analysis of real-world business data from my family's restaurant.

What began as an effort to understand the overall financial health of the business evolved into a broader business analytics project. As I worked through the data, I identified additional opportunities to explore revenue performance, cost management, product mix, profitability, and operational efficiency using different analytical tools.

Rather than recreating the same dashboard across multiple platforms, each tool is used to investigate a different aspect of the business and demonstrate a different set of analytical skills.

---

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to answer the following question:

**What does the data reveal about the health, performance, and opportunities of the business?**

To answer this question, the project has been divided into several analytical workstreams.

---

## Analysis Roadmap

### Excel: Financial Health Assessment

The Excel dashboard focuses on evaluating the overall financial health of the business.

#### Key Metrics

- Total Revenue
- Operating Expenses
- Net Operating Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Revenue by Category
- Sales Mix %
- Average Selling Price
- Items Sold

#### Business Questions

- Is the business profitable?
- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which categories contribute the largest share of sales?
- How efficiently is revenue being converted into profit?

---

### Power BI: Performance Driver Analysis

The Power BI dashboard focuses on understanding the factors driving business performance and profitability.

#### Current Areas of Analysis

- Revenue Performance
- Net Operating Profit
- Profit Margin
- Fee Burden
- Category Revenue
- Sales Volume by Category
- Expense Distribution

#### Business Questions

- What factors have the greatest impact on profitability?
- Which categories drive revenue growth?
- How much revenue is being absorbed by fees and expenses?
- Where can operational efficiencies be improved?

---

### SQL: Data Preparation and KPI Analysis

**Status: In Progress**

#### Planned Analyses

### Tableau: Executive Storytelling
